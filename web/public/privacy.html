AutoTiers Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 21, 2026

Information We Collect

When you connect an account or use AutoTiers, we may collect:

  • Your email address (from Yahoo or Google OAuth)
  • The credentials needed to access the fantasy account you connect: OAuth tokens for Yahoo and Google, your public Sleeper username, or your ESPN session cookies (SWID and espn_s2)
  • Fantasy league data including your league name and settings from Yahoo Fantasy, Sleeper, or ESPN, plus draft ADP and keeper lists where the connected provider makes them available (Sleeper and ESPN; Yahoo Fantasy does not expose live ADP or keeper data)

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Authenticate you and maintain your AutoTiers account
  • Retrieve your fantasy league settings to personalize your tier list
  • Save your scoring preferences and rule configurations across sessions

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your fantasy account data or your identity — your email address, OAuth tokens, Sleeper username, ESPN session cookies, and the fantasy league data we retrieve on your behalf — with third parties for marketing purposes.

That account and league data is transmitted only to the service providers required to operate AutoTiers: the Yahoo Fantasy, Sleeper, and ESPN APIs (to retrieve your league data), Yahoo and Google OAuth (to verify your identity), and Amazon Web Services (Amazon SES) to deliver transactional emails such as address verification, password resets, and feedback replies. Aside from these operational service providers, no other third parties receive your fantasy account data or identity.

Separately, AutoTiers shows advertising, and our advertising partners set their own cookies to do so. This is described in the “Advertising & Cookies” section below and does not involve sharing the fantasy account or league data described above.

Data Retention

The credentials you provide — OAuth tokens for Yahoo and Google, your Sleeper username, or your ESPN session cookies (SWID and espn_s2) — and your fantasy league data are stored only as long as you keep your account connected. You can disconnect any connected account — Yahoo, Google, Sleeper, or ESPN — at any time from the Connected Accounts settings inside AutoTiers. Disconnecting permanently removes the associated credentials and league data from our servers. Deleting your AutoTiers account removes all stored data.

Advertising & Cookies

AutoTiers displays ads through Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve and personalize ads and to measure their performance. This may involve processing data such as your IP address and information about your device and how you use the site.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you are shown a consent prompt (a Google-certified consent management platform) before personalized ads are served. You can accept or reject personalized advertising there, and you can change your choice later.

You can learn how Google uses data from sites that display its ads at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and you can manage or opt out of personalized advertising at google.com/settings/ads.
