AutoTiers Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 16, 2026

Information We Collect

When you connect an account or use AutoTiers, we may collect:

  • Your email address (from Yahoo or Google OAuth)
  • The credentials needed to access the fantasy account you connect: OAuth tokens for Yahoo and Google, your public Sleeper username, or your ESPN session cookies (SWID and espn_s2)
  • Fantasy league data including league name, draft ADP, and keeper lists retrieved from Yahoo Fantasy, Sleeper, or ESPN

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Authenticate you and maintain your AutoTiers account
  • Retrieve your fantasy league settings to personalize your tier list
  • Save your scoring preferences and rule configurations across sessions

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Your data is transmitted only to the services required to operate AutoTiers: the Yahoo Fantasy, Sleeper, and ESPN APIs (to retrieve your league data) and Yahoo and Google OAuth (to verify your identity). No other third parties receive your data.

Data Retention

OAuth tokens and fantasy league data are stored only as long as you keep your account connected. You can disconnect any connected account — Yahoo, Google, Sleeper, or ESPN — at any time from the Connected Accounts settings inside AutoTiers. Disconnecting permanently removes the associated tokens and league data from our servers. Deleting your AutoTiers account removes all stored data.
