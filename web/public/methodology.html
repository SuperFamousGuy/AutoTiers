How AutoTiers Builds Your Tiers

AutoTiers turns your league's scoring settings and a set of heuristic rules into a draft board grouped into tiers. Here is what happens between the settings you choose and the CSV you download.

1. Start from projections

Every player begins with a baseline projection for the upcoming season. You control how that baseline is formed by weighting the available inputs — for example prior-year production and current consensus projections — so the board reflects the sources you trust most.

2. Score for your scoring format

Projections are converted into fantasy points using your league settings: PPR / half-PPR / standard receptions, passing-touchdown value, and optional bonuses such as 100-yard rushing or receiving games and first-down scoring. The same player can rank differently in two leagues, which is the point.

3. Apply the rules you enable

On top of the raw projection, AutoTiers applies a configurable set of heuristic rules as score modifiers. Each rule nudges a player up or down for a reason a drafter would recognize — positional context, workload signals, and similar factors — and you decide which rules are active and how heavily they count. Rules you leave off have no effect on the board.

4. Convert to value over replacement

Raw points don't tell you how scarce a player is. AutoTiers compares each player against the replacement-level player at the same position for your league size — the value-based-drafting (VBD) idea — so a running back and a quarterback with similar point totals are ranked by how much of an edge they give you over what you could stream instead.

5. Cluster into tiers

Within each position, AutoTiers groups players into tiers using Jenks natural breaks, a clustering method that finds the natural gaps in the value curve rather than cutting every N players. That is why tiers have uneven sizes: a tier boundary lands where there is a real drop-off in value, so you know when it is safe to wait and when the position is about to fall off a cliff.

6. Merge into one board and export

The per-position tiers are merged into a single overall draft board, and you can download the result as a CSV to bring into your draft.

What AutoTiers is not

AutoTiers is a decision aid, not a guarantee. Projections are uncertain, rules encode conventional wisdom that can be wrong in a given season, and no tool can predict injuries or breakouts. Treat the tiers as an informed starting point and apply your own judgment on draft day.
