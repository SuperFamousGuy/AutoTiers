AutoTiers turns raw projections into draft-ready tiers using a stack of transparent, opt-in rules. This library explains each of those rules in plain English — what it does, where its thresholds come from, and what the evidence behind it actually says — alongside a glossary of the terms the app uses and answers to the questions we hear most often.
